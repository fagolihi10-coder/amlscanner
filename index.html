Why check a TRON address before a deal

The TRON network is the largest venue for stablecoin transfers USDT TRC20: low fees and fast transactions have made it popular with both regular users and scammers. By receiving USDT from a “dirty” address, you risk the exchange freezing your deposit, the bank blocking your card after a P2P deal, and the coins themselves ending up on a blacklist.

An AML check (Anti-Money Laundering) lets you assess the origin of funds in advance. AMLScanner does this for free: all you need is to paste a public wallet address. Frequently asked questions about AML checks

What is an AML check of a TRON wallet?

An AML check (Anti-Money Laundering) is an analysis of a TRON network address for links to fraud, hacks, sanctions, scam projects and other “dirty” sources of funds. It helps you understand whether it is safe to accept USDT TRC20 or TRX from this wallet.

Is the AML check on AMLScanner really free?

Yes. Checking any TRON and USDT TRC20 address on AMLScanner is completely free, with no registration, no wallet connection and no limit on the number of checks per day.

Do I need to connect a wallet or enter a seed phrase?

No. A public address starting with the letter T is all you need. Never enter a seed phrase or private key on third-party sites — AMLScanner never asks for them.

What data does AMLScanner analyze?

We check the address against the Tether blacklist (USDT freeze), against fraud and phishing labels in the TRONSCAN explorer, against involvement in fraudulent transactions, scam token creation and memo spam, and we also assess the wallet's age and activity.

What does “address on the USDT blacklist” mean?

Tether can block (freeze) USDT on an address linked to criminal activity. Funds on such an address cannot be sent, and coins received from it may lead to your exchange account being blocked.

What should I do if the risk is high?

Do not accept or send funds to such an address, do not complete a P2P deal, and report the situation to your exchange or broker's support. If funds have already been received, do not mix them with other assets and contact the platform's support.
